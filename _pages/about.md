I am <b>Panagiotis Petropoulakis</b>, a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the [Technical University of Munich](https://www.tum.de/en/). My research focuses on <b>traversability, reasoning, and representation learning for mobile manipulators</b> operating in complex and unstructured environments.

I investigate how learned state representations can encode geometric, semantic, and task-relevant properties of the environment to enable robust traversability assessment, decision-making, and motion planning. In particular, I explore methods that combine learning-based perception with structured reasoning to improve generalization and safety.

My work aims to advance autonomous operation of mobile manipulation systems in real-world domains, including construction and field robotics.

<b>Our time is limited, so we should use it wisely.</b> I choose to spend mine on four things I love: my passion for research, staying active and healthy through sports and nutritious food, connecting with family and friends, and volunteering. I am passionate about being involved in initiatives related to children interested in technology and women in tech.

My goal through my research and projects is to connect with people, both to offer and to learn from them, and ideally, to leave a positive impact.
