I am <b>Panagiotis Petropoulakis</b>, a Ph.D. researcher at the <b>Chair of Computing in Civil and Building Engineering (CCBE)</b> and the <b>Georg Nemetschek Institute (GNI)</b> at the [Technical University of Munich (TUM)](https://www.tum.de/en/). I am also a doctoral candidate at the [TUM School of Computation, Information and Technology (CIT)](https://www.cit.tum.de/), affiliated with the <b>Chair of Robotics, Artificial Intelligence and Real-Time Systems</b> under <b>Prof. Alois Knoll</b>.

My research focuses on <b>construction robotics, mobile manipulation, state representation learning, traversability, Sim2Real transfer, and long-horizon reasoning</b> for autonomous robots operating in complex and unstructured environments.

I investigate how learning-based perception, geometric and semantic representations, and structured reasoning can be combined with planning and control to enable robots to understand construction tasks, execute them safely in the physical world, verify task completion, and recover from failures.

My work spans reliable mobile-robot navigation, terrain-aware motion planning, mobile manipulation, task-aware decision-making, and the integration of digital construction information with physical robotic execution.

<b>Our time is limited, so we should use it wisely.</b> I choose to spend mine on four things I love: my passion for research, staying active and healthy through sports and nutritious food, connecting with family and friends, and volunteering. I am passionate about being involved in initiatives related to children interested in technology and women in tech.

My goal through my research and projects is to connect with people, both to offer and to learn from them, and ideally, to leave a positive impact.
